Coulee Terms of Use and Privacy Policy

Version 1 · Effective November 7, 2026

Coulee is a small, independent app for logging fishing spots and catches in Wisconsin. These terms are written in plain language. By creating an account or tapping Agree, you accept them.

Terms of Use

  1. Who can use Coulee. You must be 13 or older. Some features, such as messaging, may require you to be older. New accounts are approved by the founder before they can be used.
  2. Regulations and maps. Trout classes, regulations, seasons and public land shown in Coulee come from Wisconsin DNR data and may be incomplete or out of date. The current Wisconsin DNR regulations are what count. You are responsible for following fishing laws, having a valid license and knowing where you are allowed to fish.
  3. Private land. A creek on the map is not permission to fish it. Get permission before entering private land, and stay within easement limits.
  4. Your content. You own the photos, catches and notes you add. You give Coulee permission to store them and show them to the people you choose to share them with, and in tournaments you join.
  5. Fair play. Don't log fish you didn't catch, edit photo dates or locations to win tournaments, or reuse another angler's photos. The founder and admins can hide catches, remove tournament entries and close accounts that break these rules.
  6. Be decent. No harassment, hate, threats, spam or sexual content. Don't post other people's private information.
  7. Safety. Fishing involves moving water, weather and remote places. Use your own judgment. Coulee is not responsible for injuries, losses or fines.
  8. No warranty. Coulee is provided as is. It may have errors, go offline or change features. Keep your own copy of anything important (Me → Download a copy).
  9. Changes and ending your account. These terms may change; you'll be asked to agree again when they do. You can stop using Coulee at any time and ask the founder to delete your account and data.

Privacy Policy

  1. What we collect. Your email, name, birth year, friend code, and what you add: spots, catches, trips, photos, notes, tournaments, suggestions and an optional profile picture and bio.
  2. Locations. Your phone's location is used only when you tap Here, and only to fill in that entry. Photos are re-saved before upload, which removes the GPS data hidden inside them; the app reads that location first to fill in the form, and you can change it.
  3. Who sees what. Each catch has a setting: Exact spot, County only or Nothing. Spots start private. Friends, and people following a public profile, see only what you share. Profile pictures and names are visible to other approved members.
  4. Founder and admins. The founder can see account emails to approve accounts and protect the app. Admins can read suggestions and approve accounts but never see your private spots or exact locations. App statistics are counted in totals, without personal details.
  5. Where it's stored. Data is stored with Supabase (database and photo storage) and the app is hosted on GitHub Pages. Map data saved for offline use stays on your phone.
  6. No selling. Your information is not sold or used for advertising.
  7. Your choices. Download your data from the Me tab at any time. Ask the founder, through Suggestions, to correct or delete your account and data.
  8. Children. Coulee is not for children under 13. Accounts found to belong to someone under 13 are deleted.
